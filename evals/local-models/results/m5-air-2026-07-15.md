# MacBook Air M5 local-model qualification

Run on 2026-07-15 on a MacBook Air `Mac17,3` with an Apple M5, 10 CPU cores,
10 GPU cores, 32 GB unified memory, and the Metal backend. Noema used its pinned
llama.cpp b10015 runtime at commit
`12127defda4f41b7679cb2477a4b0d65ee6a0c8f`, an 8,192-token evaluation context,
temperature 0, one repetition, and one active generation at a time.

## Decision

Recommend **Gemma 4 E4B IT Q4_K_M** for this machine class. It passed every
critical Noema contract, had the fastest median workflow among the candidates
that did so, loaded in 1.1 seconds, and needs a 5.3 GB download. Its only miss
was the noncritical multiple-choice response shape: it returned the correct
choice content through a tool call instead of Noema's typed final response.

Offer **Qwen3.6 35B A3B UD-Q3_K_XL** as the quality-first advanced alternative.
It was the only 11/11 model, but its 16.8 GB download, 7.5-second load, and
9.1-second median workflow make it a poor onboarding default. Gemma 4 26B A4B
matched E4B's critical score but did not fix E4B's one noncritical miss; its
16.8 GB download and 12.3-second load therefore buy no observed Noema benefit.

This snapshot does not change the bundled recommendation catalog by itself.
Promotion should follow repeated qualification runs and a deliberate product
decision, especially because exact-output behavior can vary across runtime,
template, and quantization changes.

## Compatibility findings

- Google’s `gemma-4-E4B-it-qat-q4_0.gguf` aborts b10015 on both Metal and CPU
  while loading its vocabulary. The llama.cpp-maintained Q4_K_M conversion is
  the qualified artifact above; this is an artifact/runtime incompatibility,
  not a Gemma 4 family failure.
- Prism's smaller `Ternary-Bonsai-27B-Q2_0.gguf` fails b10015 with a tensor
  offset mismatch because it requires Prism's llama.cpp fork. The tested
  `Q2_g64` artifact is the stock-upstream-compatible Bonsai 27B build.
- GPT-OSS frequently placed its useful output outside the visible response
  content handled by Noema's current adapter. It needs explicit Harmony/reasoning
  channel support before it can be reconsidered; the model loading successfully
  is not enough.
