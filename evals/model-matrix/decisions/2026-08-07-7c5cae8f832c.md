## Primary recommendation correction

The OpenRouter Primary change above was withdrawn after comparison with a real
Noema conversation exposed a fixture gap. The suite graded discovery and the
grounded calendar write as separate provider calls, so a candidate could pass
both without ever composing them in one tool-using turn. Luna at low and medium
effort also failed that real flight-to-calendar request; Luna high completed it.

The shipped OpenRouter Primary recommendation therefore returned to GPT-5.6
Luna / low as the pre-decision incumbent. Suite v4 replaces the split checks
with three critical, stateful discovery-to-write workflows and evaluates Luna
low, medium, and high separately. A future recommendation change requires fresh
suite-v4 decision evidence; the other accepted changes in this record remain in
force.
