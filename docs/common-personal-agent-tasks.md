# 50 Common Personal Agent Tasks

These examples assume the agent can read email, create and modify calendar events, and read and write Notion. Email actions are read-only. Complexity reflects the breadth of context, depth of judgment, and consequences of write actions involved.

## Level 1: Direct lookups and bounded edits

Clear targets, little synthesis, and at most a narrowly scoped calendar change.

- [ ] Summarize the important emails I received today and group them by topic.
- [ ] Identify unanswered questions directed at me across recent email threads.
- [ ] Find invitations or scheduling requests in my inbox and propose suitable calendar times.
- [ ] Find the last decisions and unresolved issues related to an upcoming meeting.
- [ ] Identify overbooked days this month and suggest calendar changes.
- [ ] Find a two-hour focus block this week and reserve it on my calendar.
- [ ] Move a flexible event to make room for a higher-priority commitment.
- [ ] Add travel time before and after an in-person event.
- [ ] Add preparation time before every important meeting this week.
- [ ] Create a tentative hold for a date discussed in an email thread.

## Level 2: Extract and record

Pull structured facts or actions from a clear source and save them in another system.

- [ ] Find emails that appear to require a response and create a Notion follow-up list.
- [ ] Extract deadlines and dates from recent email and add the confirmed ones to my calendar.
- [ ] Summarize a long email thread and save the key decisions and open questions in Notion.
- [ ] Find receipts and renewal notices from this month and log them in my Notion expenses page.
- [ ] Track packages or reservations mentioned in email and add relevant dates to my calendar.
- [ ] Find newsletters I have not had time to read and create a short reading digest in Notion.
- [ ] Turn a recurring responsibility in Notion into a recurring calendar event.
- [ ] Create a meeting agenda in Notion from the questions and topics discussed over email.
- [ ] Add action items from meeting notes to the correct Notion project.
- [ ] Convert deadlines in a Notion project into calendar milestones and reminders.

## Level 3: Coordinate across tools

Connect related context and actions across email, calendar, and Notion.

- [ ] Reserve recurring time each Friday for a weekly review and link the event to my Notion review page.
- [ ] Find messages from a specific person and create a concise relationship or account brief in Notion.
- [ ] Schedule a meeting requested in an email using an available time on my calendar.
- [ ] Create calendar holds for the milestones in a Notion project plan.
- [ ] Update an event with the agenda, attendee context, and Notion notes link.
- [ ] Schedule follow-up time for action items assigned to me in Notion.
- [ ] Create a recurring meeting-notes page in Notion and link it from the calendar series.
- [ ] Turn an email thread into a meeting with a proposed agenda and preparation checklist.
- [ ] Create a Notion project page from an email thread, including the goal, stakeholders, dates, and next actions.
- [ ] Create a Notion page for an upcoming event with logistics, related correspondence, and a preparation checklist.

## Level 4: Synthesize and evaluate

Combine multiple sources, infer significance, and surface priorities or recommendations.

- [ ] Give me a morning brief with today's calendar, important new emails, and urgent Notion tasks.
- [ ] Show me what changed in my schedule, inbox, and active Notion projects since yesterday.
- [ ] Create a shutdown summary of what I completed, what remains open, and what is scheduled tomorrow.
- [ ] Build a weekly preview from my calendar, recent email, and project plans in Notion.
- [ ] Detect calendar conflicts and propose the smallest set of changes to resolve them.
- [ ] Prepare a brief for my next meeting using the invite, relevant email threads, and Notion pages.
- [ ] Build a one-page profile of everyone I am meeting today from prior email and Notion context.
- [ ] Compare this week's meetings with my stated priorities and flag low-value commitments.
- [ ] Produce an end-of-week summary of meetings, decisions, and outstanding follow-ups in Notion.
- [ ] Build a Notion contact page from my history with someone and link upcoming meetings with them.

## Level 5: Plan and maintain outcomes

Use broad context and judgment to plan work, maintain evolving state, or make several consequential changes.

- [ ] Plan my day around existing meetings, deadlines mentioned in email, and priorities in Notion.
- [ ] Turn today's Notion task list into time blocks on my calendar.
- [ ] Tell me which commitments are at risk based on upcoming events, unread email, and incomplete tasks.
- [ ] Cancel or reschedule my personal focus blocks when a deadline changes in Notion.
- [ ] Update a Notion project's status using recent emails and calendar activity.
- [ ] Collect everything related to a topic across email, calendar, and Notion into a research brief.
- [ ] Maintain a Notion decision log from decisions recorded in email and meeting notes.
- [ ] Create a travel itinerary in Notion from confirmation emails and add each reservation to my calendar.
- [ ] Review stale Notion tasks, find any relevant recent email, and recommend whether to reschedule, revise, or close them.
- [ ] Generate a monthly review in Notion covering major events, project progress, decisions, and unresolved commitments.
