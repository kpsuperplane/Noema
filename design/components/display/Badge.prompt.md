Small pill for status, counts, versions, and labels.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Online</Badge>
<Badge tone="brand">Open source</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning">Needs review</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" mono>v0.4.2</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral` `brand` `accent` `success` `warning` `danger` `info` `solid` `outline`. `dot` adds a status dot; `mono` for version/ID text.
