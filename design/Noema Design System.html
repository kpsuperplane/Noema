Elevation & motion

Shadows are warm ink-tinted (never neutral grey), layered and soft. Motion is calm — 120–200ms ease-out for UI, a slight spring on toggles and presses.

Foundations

Iconography

Lucide is the icon standard — a clean 24×24 grid with ~2px round-cap strokes, itself open source. No emoji or unicode glyphs as icons. (Substitution — confirm or supply a bespoke set.)

Brand

Voice & tone

Plain-spoken, warm, transparent. Address the user as "you"; the product is "Noema". Sentence case everywhere; wordmark lowercase. No emoji — warmth comes from tone and the clay accent.

● We say

"Noema runs on your machine. You decide what it can touch."

"Here's exactly what the agent did."

"Want a hand setting this up?"

✕ Not

"Revolutionary AI that supercharges your workflow!"

"Our proprietary engine handles everything."

"Leverage synergistic agentic paradigms."
